PREVIOUS BLOCKER RESOLUTION
===========================
AG.ADB-CONNECTION-UX-1 was BLOCKED because:
  AT v0.4.4 consumer boundary does not expose disconnect API.

Resolution for UX-1R:
  DISCONNECT = OUT OF SCOPE
  The management surface does NOT include a Disconnect button.
  ShellConnection.disconnect() (no-op placeholder) is NOT called.
  The UI only offers: Reconnect, Pair/Re-pair, Open Settings, Refresh.

  - Reconnect (state-dependent)
  - Pair / Re-pair (ALWAYS visible, click to expand)

         proper lifecycle (onResume refresh).

--- 07_pairing_ui.txt ---

PAIRING UI
==========
ENTRY: "Pair / Re-pair" button (always visible)
EXPAND: click → pairing panel appears with instructions + input
COLLAPSE: click again → "Hide Pairing" → panel hides

INPUT: numeric only, max 6, button disabled until exactly 6 digits
API: adapter.pairDiscovered(code) (AT v0.4.4 consumer)
RESULTS:
  SUCCESS → message + auto ensureReady + refresh
  REJECTED → "Pairing rejected..."
  SERVICE_NOT_FOUND → "Pairing service not found..."
  MULTIPLE_ENDPOINTS → "Multiple pairing endpoints found..."
  FAILED → "Pairing failed." + safe error
