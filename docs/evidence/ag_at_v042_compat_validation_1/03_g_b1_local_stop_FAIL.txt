Note: This is exactly the G1 gap predicted by the historical evidence
reconciliation audit. The AG-side coroutine cancellation (interruptActiveRun
-> Job.cancel) works at the AG boundary, but the AT-side LocalOneShotBackend
does not translate that cancellation into OS-level process termination.
